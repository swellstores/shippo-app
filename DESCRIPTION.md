Show your customers live Shippo carrier rates at checkout, and send paid orders to Shippo's Orders page, where you buy labels. When a label is bought, the order is fulfilled in Swell with the carrier and tracking number.

Connect Shippo with an API token, add the address your parcels ship from, and turn on Shippo in Settings → Shipping. Rates come from the carrier accounts in your Shippo account, your own or Shippo's built-in ones.

- **Live rates at checkout.** Every Shippo service, such as USPS Priority Mail or UPS Ground, becomes a checkout option with its price and estimated delivery days. Limit the carriers and services, and add a markup or a handling fee.
- **Orders ready for a label.** Paid orders, or submitted ones if you choose, go to Shippo with the address, items, weights and the service the customer chose.
- **Fulfilled when the label is bought.** The app checks for new labels every 15 minutes, or right away when you choose Check for labels now, and creates the Swell shipment with the carrier and tracking number.
- **Shippo status on every order.** A Shippo tab shows the status, carrier, tracking number, tracking page and label link, and the order list shows each order's Shippo status.
- **Send any recent order.** Send an order placed before you installed the app, or one that failed to send, with Send to Shippo on the order page.

Setup takes a few minutes. In Shippo, copy an API token from Settings → Advanced → API. In Swell, enter it in the app settings with your ship-from address and default parcel size, run Test connection, then turn on Shippo in Settings → Shipping and save.
